Gameweek 0: Initial Setup and Plan

So I've always been into Fantasy Premier League and each year I kind of lose interest part way through, injuries mount and I kind of think to myself "what is the point in this".

This year I've decided to turn it into an experiment. I have setup 3 teams and for each of the 3 teams we will be allowing a different AI asisstant to manage each of the team decisions.

The 3 I've chosen are Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot. Why these 3? I use Claude on the daily so am fairly familiar with it. Most people are aware of ChatGPT and the Premier League is sponsored by Copilot so thought it'd be be good to include.

"Why didn't you pick X AI asisstant it's the best". Time mostly and I had to find a limiting factor and chose these 3 for the reasons above.

The plan

So I gave each 3 of the AI asisstants the same prompt in a project which is listed below. I then had to in some ways "fix" the team it seleted for me on each one. Which I will detail in each section pertaining to that AI. At the end of each gameweek I will be updating this blog and detailing any issues I experience with their usage and such.

The prompt

As stated above to make it fair each asisstant got the following prompt initially:

prompt I am going to be using "AINAME" to make all my fantasy premier league decisions this year. We’re going to start by making the team. I need you to make me a team then based on that each week we will be making the allowed substitutions and such based on the current teams within the premier league.

And here are the teams which they produced and any issues I hit with each one.

The management

So to make sure I stay somewhat in control, I am going to be making the updates myself to each team there are no AI agents directly involved in accessing the sites. I will simply ask the same prompts which I will update in corresponding blog posts to manage the team, have it make suggestions on substitutions, transfers and boosts. Where possible I will be updating the reasoning behind each move. I will also be keeping the Live Tracker (driven from a Google Sheet) updated per gameweek and making a blog post where it's changed.
