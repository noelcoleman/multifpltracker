Gameweek 1: Instant Panic!

So we're underway. The scores are all in and now the actual job of managing and updating these teams is afoot.

To make managing these consistent I have a python script which scrapes the scores of each team in a consistent format and will be feeding each team their relevant file and asking them the same basic prompt and seeing where they go from there.

The prompt

As stated above I generate an identical CSV for each team, I then upload this to the relevant AI manager and give it the following prompt:

prompt This is your players points from game week X as a CSV. How would you prepare going into the next game week?

And then I track any further responses to allow the AI itself to make the decisions.

Claude

First off we have Claude who based on the performance of the previous week 3rd overall (out of 3) and fell below average with 37 points. After feeding the CSV and giving the prompt above, it singled out Haaland (MCI) as the weaker link with it's team stating that the choice to captain him effectively cost points. With the only change being suggested to change captain to Szoboszlai (LIV) as both the captain and vice captain, B. Fernandes (MUN) had poor week 1 performances. No transfers suggested at this time as "nothing in the squad screams transfer now".

ChatGPT

ChatGPT after being given it's CSV replied that we should not fall into the "Instant Panic" from Gameweek 1. But it's main concern to keep an eye on is Calvert-Lewin (LEE) as he only attained a single point bur erred on the side of suggesting an instant transfer as it would like Gameweek 2 to make a fairer assesment. It also did not suggest any transfers instead suggesting it banks the free transfer from this week giving us 2 free transfers going into Gameweek 3. ChatGPT finished 1st of the 3 teams scoring 49 points, above the average.

Copilot

Finally Copilot. Currently middle of the albeit modest pack scoring 44 points in Gameweek 1. After receiving the prompt it gave me a very good analysis on the teams overall performance, correctly identifying that the bench performed very well scoring 20 points and some of the players on the bench outscoring the starters. It did ask me what kind of profile I wanted going into week 2 until I reminded it that all decisions are for it to make. With that in mind it suggested a single non-specific transfer suggesting I change Munoz (CPY) for and I quote "a better defender". It took a few more reminders that it was in charge and being vauge about "a 6.0m defender from a top six team" if finally gave me a name Estupiñán, which gives me another issue as he moved to AC Milan this summer so I had to again correct it and it gave me Porro (TOT).

After all of this fun with the transfers it also suggested some substitutions suggesting we stick with Haaland (MCI) as captain but promoting Joao Pedro (CHE) to vice Captain. It further suggested keeping the formation with a back 3, 4 midfield and 3 attackers. Suggesting we change Van Hecke (TOT) for Aine (NFO) and swapping Gibbs-White (NFO) for Slater (HUL).
