Gameweek 2: ROLL THE TRANSFER

So we've now had 2 gameweeks and starting to see some players and managers find their feet. Will the AI managers keep their cool or decide to make moves to the tranfer market?

The prompt

I gave each AI manager the same prompt again going into week 2 as I did week 1 and as I intend to for the remainder of the weeks. I upload the relevant CSV and then give them the following prompt:

prompt This is your players points from game week X as a CSV. How would you prepare going into the next game week?

And then I track any further responses to allow the AI itself to make the decisions.

Claude

Claude has moved up to 2nd usurping Copilot by scoring an above average 88 points. Taking the total to 125. It did not however make any suggestions about changes to the personell, urging me as the title of this entry to "ROLL THE TRANSFER", instead simply suggesting moving the armband back to Haaland (MCI) owing to the fact that Szoboszlai (LIV) spent a considerable portion of the game against NFO as a makeshift right-back and I quote "which will significantly caps his cieling.". The biggest shock here is not a transfer but instead a substitution, it suggested moving Van Dijk (LIV) out for Hume (SUN), it did however give me 2 paragraphs at the same time suggesting benching Virgil as well as leaving him alone. I prompted it to ensure it was clear, I got the "Good catch, that's a contradicition." response, it still suggested I replace him with Hume in the end.

ChatGPT

ChatGPT decided today it cannot read the CSVs for whatever reason so I had to paste in the contents of the CSV for it to parse. We didn't have this issue last week so I'm chalking it up to a short term technical hitch rather than anything else. Much like Claude it didn't suggest any transfers but it did add several players to a "watch list" due to underperforming in the previous 2 weeks. Those players being Kinsky (TOT), Maguire (MUN), Slater (HUL) and Calvert-Lewin (LEE) saying that it's putting them "under the microscope". It instead suggested keeping the transfers to make meaningful improvements rather chasing one week fixture. After gameweek 2 ChatGPT is comfortably in the lead scoring 102 well above the average of 81.

Copilot

Finally Copilot. Falling into 3rd place with a below average 72 points but again having 20 points on the bench. Unlike the other 2 it did suggest a transfer wanting to replace Greaves (IPS) with another player, it made a fairly cogent suggestion> Learning from the discussion last week it instantly suggested a name, sadly the person it suggested Harwood-Bellis (AVL) is currently not registered in the FPL, so it then suggested Murillo (NFO), however those paying attention will have noticed the Copilot team already has 3 Forest players in it (also avoiding the fact it put me 1.0m over budget due to out of date pricing). Rather than prompt it I asked it "can you tell me what issues you could forsee with the CSV team I uploaded and the transfer suggestion" and to it's credit, it got it right away. I can't have another Forest player. Ultimately it suggested no further changes.

I did hit one further issue where it hadn't remembered the Porro transfer it suggested last week, instead suggesting Munoz so I had to correct this, so despite feeding it the CSV it was conflating it's memory over the information being fed.
