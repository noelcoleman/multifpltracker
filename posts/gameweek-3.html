Gameweek 3: I want to get rigorous

So now patterns are emerging, 3 gameweeks in what will our managers do?

The prompt

I gave each AI manager the same prompt again going into week 2 as I did week 1 and as I intend to for the remainder of the weeks. I upload the relevant CSV and then give them the following prompt:

prompt This is your players points from game week X as a CSV. How would you prepare going into the next game week?

And then I track any further responses to allow the AI itself to make the decisions.

Claude

Claude stays 2nd yet again scoring above average with 53. Taking the total to 178. It is however the only manager yet to make a single transfer, simply re-arranging captaincy from Haaland (MCI) to Isak (LIV) based on their upcoming fixtures and moving back in Virgil (LIV) for Maguire (MUN). It hasn't ignored the tranfer situation but simply suggests holding it and is watching B. Fernandes (MUN) but is urging caution presently.

ChatGPT

This week ChatGPT maintains a comfortable lead scoring a still above average 45 points but taking it's total after week 3 to just shy of 200 with 196 points. ChatGPT fixed the CSV issues and not only that actually impressed me by remembering it made all the decisions, it did promptly forget that though during the back and forth as it didnt simply suggest benching Maguire (MUN) it suggested replacing him as he was one on the watch list from last week. The back and forth I had with it about this transfer did lead to a fairly humourous situation. Firstly it worked out who it could replace Maguire with and suggested Guehi (MCY) but realised it couldn't afford him, so it toyed with more moves to free up the budget to do so, however it didnt want to take the points hit. So it then suggest Munoz (NFO) but with the reasoning that Crystal Palace play Ipswich, see the issue? It did after I re-prompted by pointing out he moved to Nottingham Forest, after which it said "I dont want Munoz" now which earned a slight chuckle. So instead suggested in bring in Hall (NEW) for him instead but still remonstrating that it would prefer Geuhi.

Copilot

Last so far, not just in the order of this post but in terms of the standings. Scoring second highest overall this week but still maintaining 3rd place. Copilot decided that having 3 Spurs defenders may not be the best return. It got stuck on what to do with transfers however, it looked to replace Kalimuendo (NFO) as he's only cleared a single point after 3 games so far so then suggested I replace him with Semenyo but stated (Bournemouth) which gave me 2 issues, 1 he's not a forward in FPL and 2 he plays for Man City. After this correction it suggested Solanke if we can afford him (we can't) then suggested Wood (also cant afford him) after which it finally settled on Adebayo, which was it's own set of issues firstly he played for Luton who havn't been in the premiership since 24/25 season and secondly he plays for Bristol City now. After this it then gave in making suggestions and asked for a screenshot from the app of available and affordable forwards. After supplying that it moved on to Akpom (IPS) who was at the top of the list, he's managed 6 points so far so we'll see what the return is.
