Gameweek 4: Trim the dead weight

Gameweek 4 will our managers ring in the changes? Will Claude finally make a move on transfers?

The prompt

I gave each AI manager the same prompt again going into week 2 as I did week 1 and as I intend to for the remainder of the weeks. I upload the relevant CSV and then give them the following prompt:

prompt This is your players points from game week X as a CSV. How would you prepare going into the next game week?

And then I track any further responses to allow the AI itself to make the decisions.

Claude

Claude remains second but the gap is starting to narrow considerably to just 14 points but Claude has increased it's advantage over 3rd place. This week however it identified that removing Szoboszlai (LIV) due to him not "earning" his price tag of £7.0m. It then asked if we had any additional free transfers nearly remembering that it banked them from the previous weeks, when I confirmed it had additional free transfers the next on the chopping block was van Ewijk (BRI). These players were replaced with Schade (BRE) and Mitchell (CRY) respectively. Lets see how this goes. It finally also suggested I take the armband off of Isak (LIV) in favour of Haaland (MCI) due to not having the best week during GW4. Let see if it's first dip into transfers pays off.

ChatGPT

ChatGPT is still ahead but the margin is thinning to just 14 points. ChatGPT also wanted to make a singular change wanting to replace Thomas (COV) for Nico Williams (NFO) however it had to be corrected as it had an incorrect price for him putting itself 0.6m over budget, so it decided to keep Thomas for the time being as there were no changes it identified as worth the budget hit. The only other change suggested was make the vice captin Joao Pedro (CHE) instead of Kinsky (TOT) we'll see if that makes any impact.

Copilot

I have no idea what year Copilot thinks it is, but I have a good guess as it kept asking me to swap in Luton and Burnley players who were both in the Premier League 2 seasons ago. I corrected it about Luton and Burnley but it then suggested a West Ham player. When it was corrected about this it had to go off and re-clarify the teams in the Premier League and Championship just to ensure it wasn't suggesting any further players. After all that it made 2 changes, suggesting it replaced Tosin (TOT) and Porro (TOT) due their blanking and being too reliant on Tottenham players, which then made it seem bizarre that on the list of potential replacements was Udogie. Ultimately it replaced them with Dunk and Gusto after a large number of corrections about budget concerns. For an AI integrated into the Premier League it really doesn't know whats going on. Perhaps theres a reason it's being left behind?
