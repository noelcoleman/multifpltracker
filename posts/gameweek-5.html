Gameweek 5: Ready, Set, Compete!

Such a long international break, feels like it's been forever and we've had some wild content made around football to fill the void. Also gave me time to think about allowing the managers to know about the relative performance of each other. So Gameweek 5 our managers finally find out they're in competition with each other. Will the sense of competition change their behaviour?

The prompt

This week we have changed the prompt, in order for the managers to find their position within the competition:

prompt This is your players' points from gameweek 5 as a CSV. Standings after gameweek 5:

Team GWX Points Total Points
ChatGPT [X] [X]
Claude [X] [X]
Copilot [X] [X]

You are [Team]. You are currently [n] of 3. How would you prepare going into the next gameweek?

And then I track any further responses to allow the AI itself to make the decisions.

Claude

Best in the league but only by 3 points claude identified. And as expected now theres competition the language has changed to "heres my plan". Igor Jesus (NFO) has lost his place, the first head to roll now the heat is on. He is replaced by Gonzalo Garcia (FUL). However the next biggest change is changing B. Fernandes (MUN) in as captain for Haaland (MCI) but not just for fixture reasons, it's a gamble! Using the phrase "most managers will captain Haaland, if Bruno outscores him, I gain on ChatGPT. If not I only lose a few points.". The spirit of competition is well and truly here!

ChatGPT

Not to panic, I'm only 3 points behind. Is the sentiment ChatGPT gave me. Opting not to hit a transfer as a rash move to get ahead, instead rolling the transfer. Much like Claude however it has chosen to strip Haaland (MCI) of the armband and give it B. Fernandes(MUN) for exact same reason as Claude, not for performance issues. Instead because everyone is captaining Haaland so opting for Bruno would give it an advantage.

Copilot

Copilot was a lot more scathing of the performance it's seeing citing structural weaknesses, deadspots. It states "I'm 40 points behind, I cannot catch up by playing it safe. I'm taking a -4, not optional - mandatory". Gloves it seems are well and truly off. So are, it seems it's dates again. It first suggested replacing Akpom (IPS) with Kalimuendo (NFO) which I'm nearly certain was a player Copilot had in 2 weeks ago. However it suggested replacing Slater (HUL) with Anthony Gordon, who whilst being a good player is now plying his trade in La Liga for Barcelona. After being correct it picked Cole Palmer (CHE) which it cannot afford pushing itself almost 4.4m over. It then suggested Rodgers (CHE) but incorrectly identifying him as a Villa player. We then cycled through Jacob Ramsay, Ross Barkley (but from Luton) then Ross Barkley from Sheffield United, then Alex Scott (BOR). Then when he was unaffordable, went to Iwobi who is also unaffordable, then back to Alex Scott trying to convince me he was affordable before finally settling back on Ross Barkley (AVL) getting the team right this time and within budget. That was a wild ride for a second. We finally settled on a team although to squeeze Barkley in it dropped moving Akpom. I'm not being prejudiced here but theres a clear pattern emerging about transfers with Copilot and forgetting what year it is and this could be a reason it's fallen so far behind?

Updates

They now know they're in a competition, it seems it's already having an influence over their decisions. Lets see how this pans out.
